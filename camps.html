Summer 2026

Summer Camps

Eight weeks of gymnastics and cheerleading camps with exciting themes, crafts, games, and lots of movement — Monday through Friday mornings.

Hours 8:00 AM – 12:00 PM Ages 4.5 – 12 Price $195 / week Deposit $75 non-refundable

What to expect

Gymnastics camps

Bars, beam, floor, vault, and trampoline! Kids learn basic and intermediate skills through teamwork, fitness games, crafts, and themed activities. Split into age groups for safety.

Cheerleading camps

Stunting, tumbling, cheers, dances, jumps, and more — with a performance for family and friends at the end of the week!

Pricing: $75 non-refundable deposit at registration; remaining balance charged on the first day of camp ($195 total per week).

Day Camps

Full-day program during school breaks and tracked-out weeks. Online registration only — register at least two weeks in advance.

Hours 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM Ages 5 – 12 Daily $60.00 Weekly $300.00

Registration

  • Online registration only
  • Register at least two weeks prior
  • Single daily sessions or full weeks available
  • Minimum of 4 registered campers required to hold camp

What to bring

Lunch and two snacks daily.

What to wear

Gym clothes — no belts, buckles, or zippers.

Pickup

Camp ends at 5:00 PM. A late fee applies for pickups after 5:00 PM.
