Camp information

Camps feature gymnastics and cheerleading with exciting themes, crafts, games, and lots of movement. Summer sessions run Monday–Friday mornings; school-break sessions are full-day.

Gymnastics

Bars, beam, floor, vault, and trampoline! Kids learn skills through teamwork, fitness games, crafts, and themed activities — split into age groups for safety.

Cheerleading

Stunting, tumbling, cheers, dances, jumps, and more — with a performance for family and friends at the end of the week!

Summer pricing: $75 non-refundable deposit at registration; remaining balance charged on the first day ($195 total per week).

Before you register

  • School-break camps: Online registration only — register at least two weeks in advance.
  • Single daily sessions or full weeks available (school-break).
  • Minimum of 4 registered campers required to hold a school-break camp.
  • Bring lunch and two snacks daily (school-break).
  • Wear gym clothes — no belts, buckles, or zippers.
  • School-break pickup is 5:00 PM; a late fee applies after 5:00 PM.
