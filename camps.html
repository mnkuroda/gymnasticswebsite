Summer Camps

Eight weeks of fun-filled gymnastics and cheerleading camps! Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM–12:00 PM with exciting themes and lots of movement.

Gymnastics camps (ages 4.5–12)

Bars, beam, floor, vault, and trampoline! Kids learn basic and intermediate skills through teamwork, fitness games, crafts, and themed activities. Split into age groups for safety.

Cheerleading camps (ages 5–12)

Stunting, tumbling, cheers, dances, jumps, and more — with a performance for family and friends at the end of the week!

Pricing: $75 non-refundable deposit at registration; remaining balance charged on the first day of camp ($195 total per week).
