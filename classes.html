Gymnastics Classes

Kids need activity and exercise! Gymnastics is one of the most comprehensive lifestyle programs available to children — building strength, flexibility, coordination, and confidence.

Top benefits of gymnastics

Skills learned here transfer to other sports and to life in general.

Increased muscular strength, flexibility, and endurance.

Knowledge of how to fall and roll safely for all sports.

Increased agility, coordination, and fundamentals of movement.

Discipline and self-control.

Learning to listen and follow rules and directions.

Social interaction with peers.

A confident self-image and positive attitude toward their bodies.

Self-esteem, confidence, and a positive "can do" attitude.

Learning to set goals.

Trust in one's own abilities — from "I can't" to "I can".

Bonus: It's FUN!

Classes at Young's Gym

We offer gymnastics classes from toddlers just learning to walk up to seniors in high school. Traditional classes use all Olympic apparatus — vault, uneven bars, balance beam, and floor exercise.

We also offer tumbling classes focused on floor skills, Cheer & Tumble classes, and private lessons for one-on-one instruction with our highly trained staff.

Message from the owners

We truly hope you will consider us when deciding to enroll your child in a gymnastics program! We have been family owned and operated in Wake Forest for over 30 years. We only have one facility so we only have one focus: creating the best gymnastics program around!
