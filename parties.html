Birthday Parties

If you haven't had a birthday party at Young's Gym, you don't know what you've missed! An unforgettable celebration with gym play, inflatables, and a private party room.

Party package

$325

Minimum cost for 15 children or less

  • 1½ hours total party time
  • 1 hour of play in the gym
  • 2 inflatables: 18-foot slide & 35-foot obstacle course
  • 30 minutes in party room (seats 15–20)
  • You provide food & decorations; we provide tables & chairs

Booking details

  • $175 non-refundable deposit due when your party is booked (payable online).
  • Parties are not secured until the deposit is received.
  • Balance due no later than the day of the party.
  • Plan at least a month in advance due to high demand.

Call or email the business office to verify availability: 919-554-0606 or office@youngsgym.com
