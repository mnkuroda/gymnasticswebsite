Day Camps

Full-day program for ages 5–12 during school breaks and tracked-out weeks. Structured fun, free play, group games, and more while school is out.

🕗

8:00 AM – 5:00 PM

Monday through Friday. Late fee applies for pickups after 5:00 PM.

💵

$60/day or $300/week

Register online at least two weeks prior. Minimum 4 campers required to hold camp.

🎉

What to expect

Structured fun, free play, group games, movie time, and inflatables on some Fridays!

What to bring

  • Lunch and two snacks daily
  • Gym clothes (no belts, buckles, or zippers)
