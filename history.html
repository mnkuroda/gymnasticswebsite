Our History

Young's Gym held its first class on February 28, 1994. For over 30 years we've been proud to serve Wake Forest and the surrounding communities.

A Wake Forest institution

Family owned and operated, Young's is the premier and oldest gymnastics and cheerleading facility in Wake Forest — and was the first gym in Wake County to offer both gymnastics and All-Star cheerleading in one facility.

We are proud to have been serving the Wake Forest and surrounding communities for over 30 years. It all started in downtown Wake Forest, and today we're located at 1213 South Main Street, just off the HWY 98 bypass.

Meet the owners

Alan and Shannon Young play an integral part in the day-to-day operations of the organization.

Alan was a cheerleader at NC State University and a member of back-to-back National Championship squads in 1990 and 1991. Shannon was a collegiate cheerleader at Western Carolina University and a member of the Carolina Panthers Topcats for three seasons.

Our staff
