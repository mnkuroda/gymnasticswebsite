Friday evenings

Parents Night Out

Drop the kids off for jumping, flipping, climbing, and sliding while you enjoy a few hours to yourself. Dinner without the kids sounds great!

How it works

Parent's Night Out runs on select Friday evenings from 6:30–9:30 PM. We do not hold PNO every Friday — check the events calendar for actual dates.

Online registration is required in advance through our event calendar. Walk-ins are not guaranteed and must register online at arrival if space is available. Registrations are automatically charged the admission fee when submitted.

View Events Calendar

Admission

$30

Flat fee per child — no discounts for late arrivals.

Snacks included

Two slices of cheese pizza are included in admission. You may also bring outside food and drinks for your child. We have a snack room, plus water fountains in the lobby and inside the gym.
