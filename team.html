Team & Pre-Team

For over 30 years Young's Gym has provided recreational gymnastics — and now we offer a competitive option to challenge gymnasts and help them reach their goals.

USAG Xcel program

Young's Gym debuted its first competitive team during the 2024–25 season in the USAG Xcel Bronze division. The team expanded to Silver for 2025–26, finishing top 6 of 34 teams in Bronze and top 5 of 41 teams in Silver at the NC State Meet.

For the 2026–27 season, the Youngs Competitive Team will grow to include the Xcel Gold division!

Invitation only Membership on the competitive team and pre-teams is by invitation only as spots are limited.

Pre-teams

Junior and Senior Pre-Teams are for young gymnasts who have shown exceptional strength, flexibility, natural ability, positivity, motivation, and passion for the sport.
