Policies & FAQ

Registration, tuition, makeups, cancellations, and gym policies — answers to the questions families ask most often.

Is the gymnastics program year round?

Yes. Young's Gym operates its gymnastics program year round. Once a registration for a gymnastics class is submitted, enrollment in the class is perpetual until a Drop Request Form is submitted to the office.

How do I register?

You can register online through our website using any of the “Register” links on the live class page or by clicking the “New Member Registration” button at the top of every page. If you have registered for a program at Young's in the past, use our Parent Portal (Returning Member Login).

You can also stop by the gym during office hours and we would be more than happy to register your child in person. Or give us a call during office hours and we'll register your child over the phone. A major credit card is required. For more information on how to contact us, click here.

When can I register?

Registration for classes at Young's is ongoing — you can sign up at any time. All tuition is monthly, so we will prorate your tuition if you register in the middle of a month. Young's Gym operates on a year-round basis, so classes are ongoing with no end dates.

Do you offer trial classes?

Yes. We gladly offer potential students the opportunity to try one of our gymnastics classes without any monetary expense to the parent. We do, however, require a trial class form to be filled out and submitted before a trial student is allowed to enter the gym.

Please be aware that the desired class for the trial lesson may be at full capacity, which would make it unavailable for enrollment until a spot opens up. For complete details, please contact the office.

Do you offer multi-class and sibling discounts?

Yes. You only pay full price for your first class tuition.

  • A 10% discount is given to additional children within the same family when enrolling for classes (discount is taken on the lowest priced class).
  • A 50% discount applies when one child takes multiple classes — sign up for a second class and receive 50% off that class.
Is there an annual membership fee?

Yes. Our annual membership fee is $75 per family and is charged at the time of initial registration. The annual membership fee is non-refundable. The fee is prorated throughout the course of the year:

PeriodFee
September–December$75 per family
January–May$50 per family
June–AugustNo membership fee

June–August: no Friday/Saturday classes. This fee helps defray administrative expenses including liability insurance, database fees, and more. It is only paid once during the gym's fiscal year, which starts each September.

Note: There are no membership fees for day camps, private lessons, open gyms, Parents Night Out, or birthday parties.

Do you offer refunds?

No. Young's Gymnastics & Cheerleading, Inc. has a no refund policy. If circumstances arise that keep a student from attending classes, camps, or events, credit may be issued to the student's account at the discretion of management. This credit will be good for a period of one year.

Do you offer makeup classes?

Yes. We allow patrons to schedule one makeup per month as long as their account is current, active, and in good standing. You must have paid tuition for the month you are requesting a makeup. You are not allowed to substitute makeups for unpaid or future classes. Makeups cannot be carried over from one session to another.

Not all classes may be available for a makeup if enrollment is at full capacity. Any missed class must be made up within 30 days of the missed class date, regardless of the reason for the absence (including weather or holiday closings). Otherwise, the makeup is forfeited.

Please remember that makeups are a privilege, not guaranteed — and you are only allowed one per month. Make every effort to have your child at his or her designated class time each week.

Recurring monthly payment

Young's Gymnastics & Cheerleading, Inc. operates its gymnastics class program using a monthly, ongoing registration process that runs year round. When enrolling a student in a year-round gymnastics class, enrollment is continuous and ongoing until a Drop Request form is submitted or the season ends in August.

An electronic Drop Request form must be submitted before the 25th of the month prior to the month you wish to withdraw to prevent the next month from being auto-drafted. Accounts are drafted on the 26th of each month.

Auto draft policy

When enrolling in a year-round class, your credit card on file will be automatically drafted on the 26th day of each month for the next month's classes. Example: September's tuition will be drafted on August 26th.

If you wish to withdraw from a monthly program, you must submit an electronic Drop Request form to the business office before the 25th day of the month to prevent the following month from being drafted. If not received by that time, your account will continue to be charged on the 26th for the next month.

To submit a drop request, contact the business office or use the Parent Portal.

Late fee policy

There is a $25 late fee applied to all outstanding monthly payments not received by the 2nd of the month for recreational classes, or the 8th of the month for team/pre-teams.

What is your cancellation (drop) policy?

If you need to withdraw your child from a program, let us know before the 25th day of the month by completing our electronic Drop Request form. If you forget and we have already processed payment for the next month, your child can take those last four classes, or we will credit your account for that amount (usable for one year on birthday parties, camps, open gyms, Parents Night Out, or another class if you re-enroll later).

Sending an email stating that you would like to drop a class does not remove your child from class. You are required to submit the Drop Request form to officially withdraw. Contact the office for assistance.

Can I stay and watch my child in class?

Of course! You're welcome to observe your child from our lobby.

Do you follow Wake County Public Schools' traditional calendar for spring break?

Sometimes. If the Wake County traditional calendar chooses either the week before Easter or the week after Easter to close for spring break, we will typically close that same week. If it is not one of those two weeks, we will not close the same week as Wake County traditional schools — we will typically choose to close the week before Easter.

Young's Gym email policy

When you register at Young's Gym you submit your email address with your registration. We periodically send mass emails to our customer database about upcoming programs and events. We use our in-house database and, for design purposes, outside email services.

You can unsubscribe at any time. If you receive an email from an outside service, use the “unsubscribe” link in the footer. Our in-house database requires you to reply and let us know you would like to be removed, and we will manually remove you from the database.

Since your email may be in different services, you may need to unsubscribe more than once. We are not trying to send unwanted emails.

Young's Gym inclement weather policy

In the event of inclement weather, management of Young's Gym makes the final decision on whether to open for business. We do not follow Wake County Schools' or other districts' delay/closing decisions — most of our business is conducted in the afternoon when morning weather may no longer be an issue.

There may be times when we delay or cancel morning programs but remain open for afternoon classes. Call the gym office, email us, or check the website for updates.

No matter what decision management makes, it is always your discretion whether you feel safe driving to the gym. We will always allow you to make up a class if you couldn't attend because you didn't feel comfortable driving. Your family's safety is our top priority.
