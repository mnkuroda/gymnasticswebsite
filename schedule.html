Gymnastics classes run year-round. Once registered, enrollment continues until a drop request is submitted.

Annual membership

A $75 per family annual membership fee applies when enrolling for the first time (prorated throughout the year).

Monthly tuition

Pre-School Classes

Parent participation and early skill-building for our youngest gymnasts.

Busy Bodies

Walking–3 yrs · Girls & Boys

Parent participation class with structured learning and controlled free play. Kids learn basic gymnastics positions, explore bars, beam, trampoline, and spotting equipment. The bounce house and giant swing are used in Busy Bodies only!

  • Parent or guardian must participate
  • Great prep for independent classes at age 3½
Register

Tiny Tots

Ages 3–4 · Girls & Boys

A cornerstone preschool class focusing on skills on floor, bars, beam, trampoline, and vault. Children learn basic body positions, gymnastics terminology, and social skills like listening, following directions, and staying with the class.

  • Skill development for ages 3½–4
  • Structured class environment
Register

Super Tots

Ages 5–6 · Girls & Boys

Transitional preschool class working all Olympic apparatus: floor, bars, beam, vault, and trampoline. Ideal for 5–6 year olds who may move to school-age classes or stay through age 6. Skill assessments throughout the year.

  • Bridge between preschool and school-age programs
  • Parents may consult staff on placement at age 6
Register

School Age Classes

Traditional gymnastics, tumbling, and boys conditioning on the big floor.

Girls 1

Ages 6–12

Open enrollment

Beginner gymnastics on the big side of the gym. All four Olympic events: vault, bars, beam, and floor. Warmup, three 15-minute rotations, and bars/floor weekly with vault/beam alternating.

No evaluation required.

Register

Girls 2

Ages 6–12

Evaluation required

Intermediate gymnastics with a faster pace than Girls 1. All four Olympic events with the same rotation structure. Solid understanding of basic skills required.

Email office@youngsgym.com to schedule an evaluation.

Register

Girls 3

Ages 7–12

Evaluation required

Upper intermediate gymnastics for students who have mastered Girls 2 skills. Fast-paced training on vault, bars, beam, and floor with extended focus on technique and progressions.

Evaluation required — contact the office to schedule.

Register

Jr. Advanced Girls

Ages 5–7

Invitation only

Advanced training with longer class time and four 15-minute rotations. Bars, beam, and floor weekly with vault/conditioning alternating. Requires strong basic and intermediate skills.

By invitation only. Evaluation required.

Register

Advanced Girls

Ages 7+

Invitation only

Our most advanced recreational girls class with five 15-minute rotations and extended training on all Olympic events. For athletes with strong fundamentals ready for rigorous skill work.

By invitation only. Evaluation required.
